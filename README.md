# Privacy Policy for Starship Landing

**Effective date: 20 November 2026**

Starship Landing ("the game") is a rocket landing game for Android, developed and published by **R&F Tech Solutions** ("we"). This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you play the game.

## Summary

- The game itself does not collect, store or share any personal information.
- You do not need an account, and the game asks for no permissions to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.
- The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

## Information the game does not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location. We do not run our own servers for this game, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

## Information stored only on your device

- **Game progress.** The stars you earn on each level, your lifetime number of catches and the last level you played are saved on your phone so you can continue where you left off.
- **Settings.** Whether sound and vibration (haptics) are on or off is saved on your phone so the game remembers your choice.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show a banner ad on the title screen, full-screen ads between levels and, where offered, optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device;
- device details such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
- your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) can be estimated;
- information about how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash and performance data from the ads component.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy** button on the game's title screen.

### Your choices about ads

- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under **Settings > Google > Ads** (the exact path can differ between phones).
- You can manage how Google personalises ads for your account at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- The game works without an internet connection. When the phone is offline, no ads are loaded.

## Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help where we can.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only third party that receives information through the game is Google, for advertising as described above.

## Security

Because the game does not collect personal information or send your data to our servers, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed. Data handled by Google is protected under Google's security practices.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
